Current Courses (Spring '26)

Data Structures (CPSMA-2103-01)

This course explores the design, implementation, and analysis of data structures. Understanding the logical and physical representation of data and algorithmic efficiency is strongly emphasized. Students are taught to evaluate trade-offs among different implementations for the appropriate selection of structures when solving computational problems.

Business Calculus (MATH-2613-01)

This course covers concepts of differential and integral calculus viewed through a lens for direct application in business, life, and social sciences. Topics include rates of change, optimization, marginal analysis, growth and decay modes, and area accumulation.

Ada, Oklahoma

Object Oriented Programming (CMPSC-3543-01)

This course introduces the central concepts of OOP, such as classes, objects, inheritance, polymorphism, encapsulation, and abstraction, with emphasis on reusability, modularity, and maintenance. Students explore the use of constructors, destructors, and method overriding through hands-on projects.

Internet Programming (CMPSC-3323-01)

This course introduces the principles and technologies that comprise the modern internet, and drive web application development. There is a brief review of the history leading up to the development and use of markup languages, and styling frameworks. Then, client/server side development is studied thoroughly, giving students the know how, as well as know why.

How to Play

Enter the correct sequence of numbers within the number of guesses allowed.
on Easy, the player has 6 chances to guess a 4 digit code.
on Normal, there are 4 chances to guess a 5 digit code.
on Hard, there are 3 chances to guess a 6 digit code.

With each guess entered, hints are given!
Yellow means the guess is close. + and - indicate whether the value should be raised or lowered.
